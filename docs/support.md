# Support

## Discord community

All MFSAppsControl support happens on **Discord**, in the dedicated
**#mfsappscontrol** channel (remember to pick up the role when you arrive).

That channel has a **ready-to-use template** for reporting a bug or suggesting a feature.

[:fontawesome-brands-discord: **Join the Discord**](https://discord.gg/peNzhxVPj3){ .md-button .md-button--primary }

## Reporting a problem

So that a problem can be diagnosed, please include:

- your **Windows** and **MFSAppsControl** versions (shown in [Options](options.md) → **About**);
- the **simulator** version (2024 or 2020);
- the **steps** to reproduce the problem;
- the **expected** behavior and the **observed** behavior.

## Attaching a diagnostic report

Logs save an enormous amount of analysis time.

The procedure to follow:

1. In [Options](options.md) → **Debugging**, set the **Log level**
   to **Debug** (or **Trace** if support asks you to).
2. **Reproduce the problem** — repeat exactly the steps that go wrong.
3. Still in **Debugging**, click **Export logs** and save the
   `.zip` file it offers.
4. **Attach that `.zip`** to your message on Discord.
5. Once you are done, set the level back to **Error**.

!!! info "What is in the export?"
    The `.zip` bundles **the generated logs** and your **configuration** file.<br>
    Nothing else is collected, and nothing is sent automatically: you alone decide to share it.

## Suggesting a feature

Suggestions are welcome on Discord.<br>
Describe **the need** by following the template (what you are trying to do before or during a flight) rather than the technical solution.
I can't promise every idea will be implemented, but they will all be read and considered.
